Rules & Safety

Your safety is our priority. By using a ticket, you agree to these conduct guidelines:

  • Mandatory security checks and ID verification at all designated entrances.
  • Strict no-drug policy; violators will be evicted and face legal action under the NDPS Act.
  • The organizer reserves the right to eject anyone showing intoxicated or dangerous behavior.
  • Re-entry is strictly prohibited once the ticket is scanned.
  • Organizers are not liable for personal injury or loss of property.

Food & Beverages

To maintain a secure environment, the following items are strictly prohibited:

  • No outside food, beverages, liquids, bottles, or cans are allowed.
  • Prohibited: Bags, lighters, matchboxes, tins, and flammable items.
  • Alcohol consumption is strictly limited to those of legal permissible age.

Parking

Dedicated parking zones are available at Golden Ground. Please follow the instructions of our traffic marshals.

Note: Parking fees are non-refundable, and the organizer is not responsible for any loss or damage to vehicles.

Booking & Cancellation

Tickets are non-refundable and non-exchangeable unless the performance is cancelled by the organizer. Only the ticket fee will be refunded; handling and internet fees are non-refundable.

We may collect limited personal data (name, email, phone) during booking to ensure a high-quality user experience as per our Privacy Policy.
